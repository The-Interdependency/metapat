# Glossary

## boundary

The thing between things.

## consciousness

One possible registration mode. Consciousness is not required for boundary, state, transformation, time, or registration.

## distinction

Registered differentiation.

## domain

A field of application or explication. Different domains may independently expose common structures and actions without sharing names, mechanisms, evidence standards, or laws.

## domain qualification

The rule that structural recurrence does not transfer a domain-specific term. A domain term applies only where that domain independently licenses it.

## emerge

Tensors emerge from simplexes.

## energy

A domain-qualified term. METAPAT may compare structures involving state, vector, transformation, and time with energetic structures where the applicable domain independently licenses energy; it does not call every such structure energy.

## explicationary tool

A domain term or formalism used to clarify METAPAT without redefining the root.

## observer

A simplex performing registration.

## question

A bounded unresolved state or relation available to transformation.

## record

A persistent state alteration that preserves the consequence of interaction beyond the interaction itself.

## registration

The capacity of a simplex to preserve, express, or transmit transformation.

## relate

This to that.

## relation

The result or structure of relating.

## scalar

A state metric.

## simplex

A thing with boundary and state.

A simplex may be constructed below the level at which it participates as a whole.

## state

A metricable property of a thing.

State is not measurement.

## tensor

Structure produced when simplexes relate.

A tensor is a thing and may itself become a simplex when it has boundary and state.

## thing

That which is.

## time

Sequential transformation.

## transformation

Resulting state change.

## vector

Alters state; inferred by scalar measurement.
